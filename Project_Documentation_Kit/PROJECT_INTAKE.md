# Project Intake

Ask conversationally in small groups. Record answers, basis and date. Start with the problem and first release. If the owner is unsure, explain options and tradeoffs; do not label recommendations accepted.

## Product and scope

- What problem should the project solve, for whom and in what context?
- What is the desired outcome and how will success be recognized?
- Which complete journeys must the first release support?
- What is excluded or deferred? Is there an existing phase plan?
- What happens today, and what evidence or references exist?

## Users and interaction

- Which roles use which web, mobile, desktop, CLI, API or automated surface?
- Are accounts, organizations, privileged roles or anonymous access needed?
- Who owns data, and who may read or change it?
- What design references, accessibility, languages, devices and offline needs matter?

## Business and domain

- What entities, statuses and transitions exist?
- What calculations, approvals, cancellations, limits or deadlines apply?
- Are money, bookings, sensitive data, hardware or other specialist topics involved? Ask only if relevant.
- Which rules are confirmed and which need a subject-matter decision?

## Technical and operational constraints

- Is there an existing repository, required stack, hosting target or integration?
- Are service access, hardware and accounts available?
- What performance, capacity, availability, retention and recovery expectations exist?
- What budget, team capacity, deadlines and support ownership are known?
- Are there jurisdiction-specific obligations to investigate? Do not invent obligations or claim compliance.

## Questions and assumptions

Record Q-ID, question, impact, dependent requirements/docs, blocking scope, owner and next action. Record assumptions separately with a basis and revisit trigger. Missing minor details must not block independent drafting; unanswered consequential choices are not approval.

The first reply should summarize the idea, identify confirmed choices, ask the highest-impact questions and propose the document plan. Do not require the owner to design the whole architecture before receiving assistance.
